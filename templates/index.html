About & Privacy

Study Buddy

Built by Leon Mapelera, from Zomba, Malawi.

Why I built this

I'm Leon Mapelera. I love building useful tools people can actually use — so I built Study Buddy to help people stop spending too long stuck studying the same topic. Paste in what you're learning, get a real deck back, and go. Work smart 🧠 not hard.

What this is

Study Buddy turns notes, articles, files, or photos into flashcards or quizzes, generated by AI (Mistral). Paste text, drop a URL, upload a PDF/DOCX/TXT, or snap a photo of a page — get a study deck back in seconds.

How it works

Your text (or extracted text from a file/photo/URL) is sent to Mistral's API to generate flashcards or quiz questions. Nothing is required from you to use it — no account, no email, no sign-up.

Privacy policy

What's collected: Study Buddy itself does not create accounts, does not use cookies, and does not store your notes, uploaded files, photos, or generated decks on any server after your request finishes processing (shared decks are an exception — see below).

Shared decks: if you use the "Share deck" feature, that specific deck's content is stored so the link can be opened by others. Nothing else about you is attached to it.

Third-party processing: whatever text you submit (typed, pasted, or extracted from a file/photo/URL) is sent to Mistral AI's API in order to generate your deck. That request is subject to Mistral's own privacy practices as a data processor. Avoid pasting sensitive personal information you wouldn't want processed by a third-party AI service.

Rate limiting: to keep the app free and available to everyone, requests are limited per visitor (currently 10 generations/minute, 60/hour). This is enforced by IP address temporarily in server memory, not linked to any identity, and not retained long-term.

Hosting: this app runs on Render's infrastructure. Standard web server logs (e.g. IP address, request timing) may be retained briefly by the hosting provider for operational/security purposes, separate from anything Study Buddy itself does.
