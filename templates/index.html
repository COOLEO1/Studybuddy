About & Privacy

Study Buddy

Built by Leon Mapelera, from Zomba, Malawi.

  • ✓ Free to use, no account needed
  • ✓ No sign-up, no cookies, no tracking
  • ✓ Your notes aren't stored after your deck is generated
  • ✓ Text is sent to Mistral AI to generate your deck

Why I built this

I'm Leon. I love building useful tools people can actually use. Studying the same material for too long is a drag — so Study Buddy turns it into flashcards, quizzes, or exams instead. Paste it in, get a deck, go. Work smart 🧠 not hard.

What it does

Turns notes, articles, files, or photos into a study deck using AI.

  • Paste text, or drop in a URL, PDF, DOCX, TXT, or photo
  • Choose flashcards, a quiz, or a timed exam
  • Long material (even a full play or book chapter) is handled automatically, split into sections behind the scenes

Privacy — the short version

  • No accounts. Nothing to sign up for, nothing tied to your identity.
  • Nothing stored, except decks you explicitly create a share link for — those are kept so the link keeps working.
  • Your text goes to Mistral AI (a third party) to generate your deck. Avoid pasting anything sensitive you wouldn't want an AI service processing.
  • Rate-limited by IP (10 requests/min, 60/hour) to keep the app free for everyone. This isn't linked to who you are, and isn't kept long-term.
  • Hosted on Render, which may briefly log standard server data (like IP address) for security — separate from anything Study Buddy itself does.
